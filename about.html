04.0 — Direct-to-Source Commercial Procurement

About Pactform

We're a sourcing platform for commercial contract furniture, built for restaurants, hotels, offices, and hospitality projects that need more than a retail catalogue.

Our Mission

What we do

We connect your project directly with the people who make it, and stay with it until it's delivered—eliminating unnecessary middlemen.

Engineered for Public Spaces

Contract furniture is commercial-grade: purpose-built and tested for heavy daily use in public and business spaces. It must meet structural, safety, and durability standards that ordinary retail furniture simply cannot support.

We work with a vetted network of trusted manufacturers across Asia, ensuring projects get finish consistency, replacement availability, and reliable lead times—without distributor markups or limited showroom catalogues.

Integrated Capabilities

How we're set up

Sourcing, engineering, procurement, and logistics support—coordinated under one roof to protect your project timeline.

01 / Sourcing

Vetted Manufacturing

Direct partnerships with trusted fabrication facilities, carefully matched to your project's specific scale, material demands, and budget standards.

02 / Engineering

Technical Integrity

Production drawings, mechanical specifications, and structural joint details checked and verified before anything goes into mass production.

03 / Procurement

Managed Execution

Transparent factory pricing, production lead-time scheduling, and multi-stage quality control inspections managed entirely on your behalf.

04 / Logistics

Site Fulfillment

Export packing, export/import documentation, customs clearance, and freight delivery coordinated directly through to your site.
