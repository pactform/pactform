ABOUT PACTFORM

Pactform is an international furniture company providing distinctive contract furniture and project furnishing solutions for restaurants, hotels, workplaces and other commercial interiors.

OUR APPROACH

DISTINCTIVE BY DESIGN. READY FOR THE PROJECT.

We combine furniture with character, custom capability and coordinated project support; from selection and specification through production and delivery.

INTEGRATED PROJECT DELIVERY

FROM BRIEF TO DELIVERY

Pactform coordinates product selection, custom furniture development, production oversight, quality control and international delivery requirements through one structured process throughout your project.

01 SELECT

PROJECT REQUIREMENTS DEFINED

We align the furniture mix, dimensions, materials, finishes, quantities and schedule with the needs of your space and project.

02 DEVELOP

DRAWINGS AND SAMPLES APPROVED

Custom and adapted pieces are developed through drawings, material selections and samples for review and approval before production begins.

03 PRODUCE

CONSISTENCY THROUGH PRODUCTION

Our furniture is produced through selected international manufacturing partners under our specifications and quality controls, with reviews at key production stages.

04 DELIVER

PACKING AND FREIGHT COORDINATION

Packing, export documentation and freight are coordinated around the project location, program and agreed delivery scope.
