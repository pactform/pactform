DELIVERY SCOPE

WHAT WE COORDINATE

Pactform coordinates export packing, shipping documentation, international freight and destination delivery according to your project requirements and agreed scope.

01

PACKING : PROTECTED FOR TRANSIT

Pactform furniture is packed according to its materials, construction, shipping route and freight requirements.

02

DOCUMENTATION : PREPARED FOR SHIPMENT

We prepare commercial invoices, packing lists and applicable export documents for each agreed shipment.

03

FREIGHT : MATCHED TO THE ORDER

Sea freight typically serves commercial furniture orders, while air freight may be used for samples, replacements and urgent smaller shipments.

04

DESTINATION : COORDINATED TO THE AGREED POINT

Customs support and final-mile delivery can be coordinated where available and included in the project quotation.
