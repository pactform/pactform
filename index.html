WHY PACTFORM

Pactform is an international furniture company providing distinctive contract furniture and project furnishing solutions for restaurants, hotels, workplaces and other commercial interiors.

Unlike furniture intended primarily for the home, contract furniture must account for frequent use, consistent specifications, project quantities and future replacement needs. We select and develop our furniture accordingly, with production through selected international manufacturing partners under our specifications and quality controls.

WE STARTED PACTFORM FOR ONE SIMPLE REASON.

Finding commercial furniture shouldn't be harder than designing the space itself.

Too often, businesses are limited by local catalogues, long distributor chains, and products that look identical from one supplier to the next. Yet behind many of these collections are talented manufacturers capable of far more than what's available locally. Pactform was created to bridge that gap.

We connect projects directly with trusted manufacturers, making commercial furniture sourcing simpler, more transparent, and more flexible.

Whether you're furnishing a café, a hotel, an office, or an entire development, our goal is always the same: help you find the right furniture — not simply sell you what's available.
